Smart Construction Lab is recruiting qualified graduate students

If you are interested in being part of the research team at Smart Construction Lab, please send your CV to Prof. Byungjoo Choi (bchoi@ajou.ac.kr). Prospective students should have skills or strong interests in the following topics or areas:

  • Construction Engineering and Management
  • Wearable Technologies
  • Human Aspects in Construction Projects
  • Construction Safety and Health Management
  • Computer Simulations
  • Building Information Modeling
  • Smart Building and Construction
  • Off-Site Construction
  • Construction Robotics
