About SCL

The Smart Construction Lab (SCL) at Ajou University focuses on developing advanced digital and automation technologies to improve productivity, safety, and quality in construction projects. Our research integrates artificial intelligence, computer vision, robotics, and BIM-based methodologies to enable data-driven and intelligent construction processes.

If you have an interest in working with SCL, please contact the Director, Prof. Byungjoo Choi (bchoi@ajou.ac.kr).
